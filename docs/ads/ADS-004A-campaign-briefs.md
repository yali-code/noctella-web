# ADS-004A — Campaign briefing (human-review only)

Campaign drafts are derived from the existing ADS-002C eligible-review projection, which itself relies on the authoritative ERP, stock reservations, Marketing Tags, external listings and recent local inventory observations.

This is a **deterministic planning engine**, not a claim that generative AI has produced validated ad copy. It produces compact creative briefs and keyword hints from source tags. No unverified condition statements, scarcity promises, buyer demographic profiles, platform audience claims, synthetic purchases or expected ROAS.

The draft links to the existing eBay/Etsy listing; objective is marketplace traffic. All output requires human review; it cannot send anything to Meta, Google or Pinterest. Budgets are handled by ADS-004B. Real AI-generated copy may be layered later with evidence grounding and an approval gate.

Phase 4 activation is still blocked pending verified advertising accounts, privacy review, live listing recheck and a provider-side pause/cancellation policy.
